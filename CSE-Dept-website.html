Where curiosity becomes capability

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering prepares students for careers in software development, data science, artificial intelligence, cyber security and research. Our curriculum is reviewed regularly with industry experts so that what you learn in class matches what employers need.

Every student works on projects from the first year, joins technical clubs, takes part in hackathons and completes internships before graduating.

Our vision

To be a centre of excellence in computing education and research that produces skilled, ethical and innovative engineers.

Our mission

To deliver quality teaching, encourage research and projects, and build strong links with industry and society.

Your path to a job

  1. Year 1 to 2: programming, aptitude and communication skills.
  2. Year 2 to 3: data structures, projects, coding contests and certifications.
  3. Summer of year 3: internships with partner companies.
  4. Year 4: mock interviews, resume clinics and campus drives.
  5. Offer: join a company or move to higher studies.
